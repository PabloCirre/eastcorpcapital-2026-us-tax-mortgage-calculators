## 2.1 S-Corporation Reasonable Compensation, FICA Cap ($184,500) & IRC § 199A QBI

When a profitable LLC elects Subchapter S taxation (Form 2553 / Form 1120-S), net profits are split into **W-2 Reasonable Officer Compensation** (subject to FICA payroll taxes) and **Schedule K-1 Shareholder Distributions** (exempt from 15.3% self-employment/FICA tax).

In tax year **2026**:
- The **Social Security (OASDI) 12.4% wage base cap** is **$184,500** ($22,878 maximum OASDI tax).
- Above the IRC § 199A taxable income phase-out threshold, the **20% Qualified Business Income (QBI) deduction** for non-SSTB companies is limited to the greater of **50% of W-2 wages** or **25% of W-2 wages + 2.5% of UBIA**.
- Setting W-2 salary at **28.57% (2/7ths) of total pre-salary net profit** mathematically maximizes the 20% QBI deduction while minimizing FICA drag.
