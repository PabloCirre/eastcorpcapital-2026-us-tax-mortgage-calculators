- **NYC Mansion Tax (8 Tiers):** Real estate closing calculation for purchases over $1M up to $25M+.

- **NY LLC Section 206 Albany Publication Arbitrage:** Legal cost reduction from $1,650 down to $215.
- **New Jersey Exit Tax (Form GIT/REP-1):** Withholding rule comparison (2% gross consideration floor vs. 10.75% net gain).
- **Pennsylvania Inheritance Tax (Form REV-1500):** Statutory 5% early-payment discount within 90 days.
